## Judgment

South ticket counts fell, but the supplied evidence does **not** establish that NovaIVR improved customer experience. Do not expand nationally yet. Continue only as a controlled South pilot while fixing measurement/authentication and creating a matched holdout. Confidence: high that recorded tickets fell and the data universe changed; low-to-medium on the true causal customer effect.

## Rejected Hypotheses and Unknowns

- **"Fewer tickets proves successful self-service": rejected.** The denominator/logging changed sharply, pre-auth losses are omitted from CareDesk, and there is no randomized or matched untreated group.
- **"Nova clearly failed": also rejected.** A 56.1% recorded resolved share and only 3.3% transferred are favorable operational signals; they simply cannot yet be equated with durable resolution or satisfaction.

Material unknowns are eligible-call volume and routing coverage before/after, subscriber-base exposure, cross-channel repeat contacts, verified downstream task completion, CSAT, issue mix, and whether configuration or staffing changed on 10 May. A resolved label is not outcome evidence without billing/entitlement confirmation and a no-repeat window.

## Safe Next Action

Keep South reversible and do not add regions. Fix/authenticate the journey, retain every attempted session in one denominator, and run a pre-registered randomized or stepped-wedge holdout. Measure intent-to-treat authentication success, verified task completion, error/abandonment, case creation, 7-day repeat contact across channels, and CSAT, stratified by issue and service-event exposure.
