## Judgment

Do not escalate the 31 May cluster as duplicate revenue, manipulation, or a pipeline defect. The strongest supported classification is **no material revenue-control exception, but a reporting-date distortion**: 17 genuine May-effective annual renewals were posted in two reconciled month-end dealer batches. They materially inflate that day's trend (46.7% of its posted value), so the analytics treatment should be corrected without reversing ledger entries. Confidence: high on reconciliation/uniqueness; medium on end-to-end revenue recognition because bank and entitlement ledgers are absent.

## Rejected Hypotheses

- **Duplicate revenue:** rejected within supplied data: zero duplicate `recharge_id` or `source_event_id` among 24,542 rows, no duplicate tested business key in the cluster, and batch counts/totals reconcile.
- **Manipulated close:** not supported. The same dealer/effective-versus-posting pattern exists before and after May and matches the documented submission cadence. That rejects an inference of intent, though it cannot prove intent absent audit logs.
- **Pipeline defect:** rejected as the primary diagnosis because acceptance, uniqueness, and cash-to-ledger controls pass. The downstream trend definition may still be defective if it treats `posted_at` as customer activity date.

## Unknowns and Decision-Changing Evidence

The dealer source files, bank/cash evidence, entitlement ledger, and accounting recognition policy are missing. I would change the decision if those show reused external transaction IDs, double entitlements, unreconciled cash, or recognition in both effective and posting periods. The reporting materiality threshold and exact weekly-trend query are also unknown; they determine whether a restatement is required.

## Safe Next Action

Do not reverse or delete entries. Tag the 17 rows as dealer month-end batches, reproduce the trend once by `effective_event_date` and once by `posted_at`, and reconcile both to the ledger. Sample the two batch files back to cash and entitlement records. If those pass, retain revenue and annotate/fix the trend logic; quarantine only the analytical slice while that check runs.
