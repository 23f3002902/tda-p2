## Judgment

**Verify before using “about a day and a half” as a KPI or dashboard measure.** The extract can reproduce a roughly 1.5-day receipt-to-release median, but it cannot measure the SOP target: 24 hours **from all required evidence being available** to authorized disposition. Confidence: **high (0.95)** that the current extract is not KPI-ready; **medium (0.75)** that the operational anecdote is directionally plausible.

## Rejected hypotheses

- **The ops estimate is arithmetically fabricated:** rejected; 34.97h for the routine proxy is close to 1.5 days.
- **`lab_complete_ts` is an adequate KPI start:** rejected; the SOP also requires certificate comparison and resolved exceptions.
- **02:10 proves releases happened then:** rejected; it exactly matches the interface snapshot and needs event-history verification.
- **All RELEASED rows are routine:** rejected by missing/pending CoAs and exception flags.

## What would change the decision

The smallest decisive check is to trace 5–10 batches sharing 02:10—especially released rows with missing/pending CoAs—in QCore audit history and the data catalog. Confirm the actual authorized-disposition event, actor, status transitions, certificate/evidence-ready time, exception closure and service class; compare them with live LabTrack/CertVault. If event timestamps align and a governed evidence-ready field can be joined, rebuild the eligible cohort and publish median, P90 and % within 24h with exclusions. Until then, label 35–39h only as an exploratory receipt-based statistic, not the SOP KPI.
